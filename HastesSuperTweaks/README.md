# Haste's Super Tweaks

Opinionated and highly optimized quality-of-life tweaks for WoW Forever in one neat package.

Built for WoW Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001); the TOC also lists Classic Era's 11509.

Modules that act for you or take over part of the game's interface (Upgrade Spell Ranks, Auto Accept & Turn In Quests, Skip NPC Chat, Hide Quest Tracker, Loot & Vendors, Consumable Macros, Auto-Equip Bags, Bag Tweaks, Auto Accept Summons, Auto Accept Resurrection, Auto Release in Battlegrounds, Chat Spam Filter), the Auto-Decline modules (except Auto-Decline Guild Charters), Better Scrolling Combat Text, Better Breath Meter, Error Message Filter, Game Settings and the Sound modules start off; everything else starts on. Every module can be turned on or off at any time without a `/reload`. Which modules are on is shared by all characters.

## Settings window

`/hst` opens it. It is also listed under Options › AddOns and in the minimap addon menu. Drag the title bar to move it, the bottom right corner to resize it, and use the − / + beside the close button (or the mouse wheel over them) to scale it; it opens the size, scale and place you left it.

### Sidebar

Search is at the top, then every tweak by category. Click a category to open or close it; its count says how many of its tweaks are on. Profiles and About are at the bottom. The version beside the title opens the patch notes; a blinking dot on it means you haven't opened the newest ones yet (New Patch Dot, at the top of the patch notes, turns it off). Each version there folds open and shut, and a change with an arrow opens its tweak's settings.

### All tweaks

Every tweak with a switch to turn it on or off, filtered by All, On or Off. Pointing at one shows its description and preview on the right; clicking it opens its page.

Tweaks with nothing to set (Kills to Level, Quest XP in Quest Log, Screenshot Level-Ups, Skip NPC Chat, Quest Item Sparkle, Quest Reward Value, Right-Click Guild Invite, Auto-Decline Battle.net Friend Requests, Minimap Button, Hide Issue Reporter) have no page. They're just a switch, in the list and in the sidebar, and clicking them turns them on or off.

### A tweak's page

The page has the tweak's switch, tabs for its settings pages (and chips for sub-pages), and the settings in cards, each with its description under it. Sliders take a typed value too. A live preview sits beside the settings (above them for the XP bar) and redraws as you change anything: the XP bar with its labels at its real size, the loot window both as you loot by hand and as auto looting lists it, the tooltip, combat text, breath meter and the rest. "Hide preview" gives the settings the whole width. A tweak that's off shows its settings, disabled, until you turn it on.

### Search

Search finds tweaks and settings by name, description, section, page or menu choice; every word must match. Settings whose name matches come first. A toggle can be switched right in the results; others show their value. The arrow keys move through the results and Enter opens one, highlighting the setting on its page.

### Profiles and About

Profiles lets you switch, create, copy, delete and reset profiles. Profiles hold every tweak's settings; statistics are saved per character. About lists the commands and credits. The GitHub button sits beside Profiles and About.

### Other details

Settings that cannot apply are disabled. Font menus preview each font. A confirmation accepted after you switched profiles does nothing, since it was asked about the other profile. Profiles made on one character are listed on all of them. Closing and reopening the window returns to the page you had open. Changes take effect immediately. The Loot Window's "Show on screen" shows its sample where the window opens, to drag it into place.

## Commands

- `/hst`: settings window. `/hst help`: command list.
- `/hst xp`, `/hst tooltip`, `/hst sct`, `/hst breath`, `/hst ranks`, `/hst macros`, `/hst mail`, `/hst clutter`: XP Tracker, Better Tooltips, Better Scrolling Combat Text, Better Breath Meter, Upgrade Spell Ranks, Consumable Macros, Mail Expiry & Alt List and Sell Clutter Automatically settings. Any module's internal name works too, for example `/hst guildmotd`.
- `/hst train`, `/hst tracker`, `/hst rolls`, `/hst restock`, `/hst mailguard`, `/hst bags`, `/hst spam`, `/hst game`: Train All, Hide Quest Tracker, Movable Loot Rolls, Restock at Vendors, Mail Gold Guard, Bag Tweaks, Chat Spam Filter and Game Settings settings.
- `/hst camp`: Campfire Helper settings. `/hst camp check`: your campfire, when you can light another, your camp buffs and Boosted Rest.
- `/hst keep <item>`, `/hst sell <item>`: put an item on Sell Clutter's Never sell or Always sell list (shift-click it into the chat line); again takes it off. `/hst clutter list`: what Sell Clutter would sell right now.
- `/hst sort`: clean up your bags (the game's Clean Up Bags).

XP bar commands use `/hxp`:

- `/hxp`: XP Tracker settings. `/hxp help`: command list.
- `/hxp lock`, `/hxp unlock`, `/hxp center`: position the XP bar.
- `/hxp preview`: sample XP data for styling the bar at any level.
- `/hxp resetstats`: restart the XP/hour session.
- `/hxp show`, `/hxp hide`: turn the XP Tracker on or off.

## Installing

Unzip the release into your game's `Interface\AddOns` folder, so that you get `Interface\AddOns\HastesSuperTweaks`, then restart the game. Releases are on the project's GitHub page: https://github.com/hastecraft/super-tweaks

## Limits and edge cases

What each module does in unusual situations, and what the game doesn't let addons do.

### Leveling

- XP Tracker takes one settled XP reading per level-up, no work while hidden, and per-character data saved under the real name even when the client says "Unknown" while loading (on WoW Forever, first name and surname, so characters sharing a first name keep their own). XP per hour and time to level show "--" for a session's first minute. In dungeons and raids, where the game hides AFK status from addons, "Pause while AFK" goes by the last status it saw. With your XP turned off the bar says "XP OFF". "Hide Blizzard XP bar" hides only the Experience bar, not reputation or honor.
- In Edit Mode the XP bar shows even when its rules would hide it, and snaps to the grid while the grid is shown (Shift places it freely). Addon frames can't join Edit Mode's settings dialog without tainting it, so right-click the bar for its settings. Positions are kept per layout unless "Position per Edit Mode layout" is off; a renamed layout keeps its position.
- Kills to Level reads kill XP from the combat XP message, minus the rested bonus. A kill of the same mob at the same level and group size gives an exact count; anything else is the formula's estimate, marked "~". Pets, totems and guardians get no line.
- Quest XP in Quest Log and the XP bar's quest shading use the game's reward when it's above 0, else Questie's estimate (marked "~"). Forever reports 0 XP for every quest in the log, so without Questie no quest XP shows. Titles update the next time the log redraws; forcing a redraw could taint the quest log.
- Upgrade Spell Ranks only acts on a rank you just learned, and only out of combat with nothing on your cursor (a rank learned in combat goes on your bars when it ends). Macros and items are never changed, and a button is never swapped down. Ranks learned while it's off aren't caught up; use "Upgrade all buttons now".
- Train All learns one thing at a time, the last one listed first, and waits for the trainer to confirm each; it stops when you leave the trainer, when combat starts, or when the trainer doesn't teach one. New professions (which ask you first) and pet training are left to you, and so is anything the trainer's filter hides. The level-up line reads your spellbook, which lists the spells you haven't learned with the level they come at; a spell it doesn't list there isn't named.
- Campfire Helper goes by your own casts and auras, since the game has no campfire API: lighting a Basic, Journeyman or Expert Campfire Kit, Welcoming Campfire (the minute you sit), Camp Benefits (the camp buffs' hour) and Boosted Rest. The fire's 15 minutes are timed from when you light it, so a fire you pack up or that burns out early isn't noticed, and other players' fires aren't timed. Camp buffs are the buffs that last as long as Camp Benefits and end with it. Auras are hidden from addons in combat, so what changed then is said once combat ends.

### Quests, NPCs and group

- Auto Accept & Turn In Quests leaves you quests with a choice of rewards, quests that cost gold or flag you for PvP, repeatable quests, and NPCs with special gossip options. It doesn't accept a share Auto-Decline Shared Quests is declining, or an escort while your quest log is full or you're dead.
- Skip NPC Chat only picks an NPC's single option when it opens a banker, battlemaster, guild charter, tabard, flight, trainer or vendor window. Plain chat, spirit healers, innkeepers and talent unlearning are never chosen.
- Quest Reward Value compares what each reward sells for (times how many you get); rewards whose details haven't arrived yet are compared once they do. It never chooses for you, and Auto Accept & Turn In Quests still leaves quests with a choice of rewards to you.
- Hide Quest Tracker moves the tracker under a hidden frame rather than collapsing it with the game's own code, which would lay it out again from this addon (its quest item buttons included); so hiding and showing wait for combat to end, and In combat hides it as combat starts. Showing only the current zone's quests isn't offered: it would mean changing which quests you watch.
- Auto Accept Summons checks again when its countdown ends that it's the same summon and that your settings still accept it. A summon in combat is counted down once combat ends; while you can't teleport (falling, say) it waits.
- Auto Accept Resurrection waits out the corpse recovery delay, then checks the offer, Shift and the combat setting again. Hardcore realms offer no resurrection.
- Auto Release in Battlegrounds doesn't release while a soulstone, Reincarnation or someone's resurrection is available, and releases after a `/reload` while dead.
- Group Finder Whispers is on only while you (or your group) have a Group Finder listing. The level shows when the game knows it for the sender. Whispers the game hides from addons (in dungeons and raids, where chat is restricted) aren't tagged.

### Loot and vendors

- Sell Junk and Sell Clutter share one sale per vendor visit, one item every tenth of a second: greys first, then clutter, so the Buyback tab holds the clutter. Each item is checked again just before it's sold, a dropped sale is retried once, and only sales that went through are reported. Both skip bags you've set to ignore junk selling and stop when you leave the vendor. Sell Clutter recognizes Classic Era's materials by item ID, since Era files nearly all of them as plain Trade Goods.
- Repair Automatically reports a repair once the game shows it happened. With guild funds on, the game pays what your guild allows and takes the rest from your gold. Short of gold, it waits while you're at the vendor, so junk sold there can pay for it.
- Loot Window hands looting to the game's own window while you're the master looter, since the master loot menus need it. Escape closes the window while it has loot to take; the auto loot list leaves Escape alone. The burnt page's art only loads once that style is used.
- Movable Loot Rolls moves the rolls right after the game lays out the column of frames above your action bars, where they otherwise stack, so that column still keeps their room while rolls are up. Rolls already up when you reset the place go back with the next roll. With a gamepad the game shows its own rolls.
- Sell All Junk without asking (Sell Junk Automatically) answers only the confirmation of the vendor's own Sell All Junk button.
- Restock at Vendors buys in the vendor's own bundles (arrows come by the 200), so it can end up to a bundle above the amount you set. Items that cost something other than gold are skipped; it stops at your gold limit, when your bags are full or the vendor runs out, and says what arrived. The low ammo warning only watches while you have a bow, gun or crossbow equipped and use ammo.

### Items and mail

- Consumable Macros recognizes items by their use spell, in the game's language, so new items work without a list; Healthstones are known by item ID. The macros are account-wide, use items by ID, and are only rewritten when their text changes: never in combat, while you're dead, or while the Macros window is open. In combat Eat & Drink leaves out the mana potion after a healing potion, since potions share a cooldown.
- Mail Expiry & Alt List only knows a character's mail once you've opened its mailbox. On WoW Forever characters are told apart by first name and surname, and the list gives the full name the To: field takes; characters saved before this addon knew surnames are dropped, and each comes back when you log in on it. An inbox holding more than it can show is reported too, since that mail's expiry can't be read until some is taken out.
- Mail Gold Guard only marks mail a player sent (mail you can reply to): auction house, quest and other game mail, and mail coming back to you, never are. Your characters count once this addon has seen each (logged in with Mail Expiry & Alt List on, or at a mailbox); friends, Battle.net friends and guildmates are matched by name and realm. It lowers the risk of taking gold you didn't ask for but can't promise anything: taking marked gold by hand, or returning it, stays your choice.
- Bag Tweaks moves only the combined bags (Options > Controls > Use Combined Inventory Bags), right after the game places them each time they open; the bag frames aren't protected, so this works in combat. Its Clean Up button only shows when the game's own isn't on the combined bags. The Clean Up and new-item settings are the game's own, which WoW Forever's options don't show.
- Auto-Equip Bags only acts on bags that arrive (looted, bought, or from mail, trade or the bank), never on bags you already had or took off yourself. Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags are never equipped or replaced. It waits out combat and anything on your cursor, and doesn't retry a swap the game refused.

### Social and Auto-Decline

- Right-Click Guild Invite only adds to name menus (chat, channels, communities); unit-frame menus, which hold protected actions, are left alone.
- Guild Message Popup can't read the message in dungeons and raids, where chat is restricted, and shows it at the next chance.
- Chat Spam Filter only looks at public channels: trade, general, local defense and looking for group, and also channels players made, and say and yell, when you choose. A message is hidden when it contains a word or phrase of a list that's on, in any case, unless it's from you, a friend or a guildmate. Messages the game hides from addons (in dungeons and raids) pass through untouched.
- The Auto-Decline modules turn everyone down unless you let them through on the shared "Let through" section: friends (including Battle.net friends), guild members and community members, all off by default. Characters are matched by GUID, or by name and realm, so a stranger with a friend's name on another realm is still turned down. On WoW Forever, where names are unique across the region, the whole name with its surname decides. While chat is locked down (in some instances) the game hides community members, so they count as strangers then.

### Interface

- Better Tooltips works on Blizzard's tooltip and on skinned ones (when another addon hides the border, it draws its own). The guild line replaces a rank another addon added, such as EllesmereUI's. Guild notes come from the guild roster, read at most every 15 seconds; in dungeons and raids, where the game hides the roster, the last notes stay. Tooltips at the mouse covers the tooltips the game places in its default spot. The target line and NPC IDs leave out units the game hides from addons, and the target line shows when the tooltip is drawn, not as the target changes.
- Elite & Rare Markers marks only the game's own nameplates: a nameplate addon that replaces them draws its own. A rare is announced once a session, when its nameplate first shows (turn on enemy nameplates); one whose identity the game hides (in instances) isn't.
- Error Message Filter uses the game's own list of error types its error frame leaves out, so it costs nothing per message; spell failures the game reports under a general error type can't be told apart, and still show.
- Game Settings holds each setting the way Better Scrolling Combat Text does: what it was is kept, and put back when you switch it off, unless you changed it yourself meanwhile. Settings that can't change in combat wait for it to end. The action camera is the game's ActionCam command; while on, the game's Keep Character Centered (part of Motion Sickness) is off, and the game's experimental feature warning is confirmed for you. Sound in Background and Error Speech aren't here: they're in the game's Options > Audio. The Edit Mode backup holds this character's and the account's layouts; restoring reloads the interface, and a backup at login is skipped while you have no layouts of your own, so losing them never replaces a good backup.
- Better Scrolling Combat Text can't read the combat log, which this client closes to addons, so it can't tell which spell hit you or who hit an enemy: numbers on nameplates are everyone's damage to that mob. Mobs tapped by players outside your group are skipped. The game draws nameplates one whole nameplate at a time, so a number can only sit under a nameplate as part of one drawn earlier; by default numbers go behind every nameplate ("Draw numbers" changes that). With "The game's own numbers" chosen for enemies, the game draws its damage numbers itself, so only their font and size change: the font from your next login (the game loads it only then), and the size with the game's other floating text, XP gains included. While it replaces the game's combat text, the game's own frame stops showing what this shows; notices you turn off here still show the game's way, and XP gains stay the game's. The CVars it changes are put back when you turn it off, unless you changed them yourself meanwhile.
- Hide Issue Reporter waits for the reporter to load, and for combat to end if it's protected. Only beta and test clients have the reporter; on others the tweak is unavailable.

### Sound

- Mute Custom Sounds' Rain and weather switch mutes Classic Era's weather loops and the newer rain, thunder and lightning sounds WoW Forever plays with its weather; thunder other storm sounds share is muted with it. The spell fizzle switch mutes the fizzle of every school of magic.
