# Haste's Super Tweaks

Small quality-of-life modules for WoW Forever (1.60.1, interface 16001; the TOC also lists Classic Era's 11509), with one settings window.

## Modules

| Module | Category | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XP Tracker | Leveling | An XP bar that shows your rested and quest XP, how fast you're leveling, and how long until your next level. Move it in Edit Mode, where it snaps to the grid and remembers a position per layout. |
| Kills to Level | Leveling | Mob tooltips estimate how many more kills of that mob you need to reach your next level. Predictions are marked with `~`, including those based on recorded kills. |
| Quest XP in Quest Log | Leveling | Your quest log shows how much XP each quest is worth, and how much of a level that is. |
| Screenshot Level-Ups | Leveling | Takes a screenshot each time you level up, once the "Level N" banner is on screen. |
| Upgrade Spell Ranks | Leveling | When you learn a new rank of a spell, your action bars get it in place of the old one. Lower ranks you put on a bar on purpose are left alone. |
| Auto Accept & Turn In Quests | Quests & NPCs | Accepts quests and turns them in for you when you talk to quest givers. Hold Shift to do it yourself. |
| Skip NPC Chat | Quests & NPCs | Opens flight maps, bank, vendor and trainer windows right away when that's all an NPC has to offer. Hold Shift to see the chat. |
| Quest Item Sparkle | Quests & NPCs | Keeps the sparkle on quest items and objects you can loot or use in the world, even with the game's outlines turned off, by turning on the game's `outlineModeShowLootEffectWhenDisabled` option each time you enter the world. Turning it off puts the option back. |
| Loot Window | Loot & Vendors | Replaces the loot window with a compact list in this addon's style, with a Take All button and hotkey. While you auto loot, it lists what you picked up for a few seconds instead. Move it in Edit Mode. |
| Fast Loot | Loot & Vendors | Takes all the loot at once instead of one item at a time, whenever the game auto loots. Moderate speed (the default) waits a frame so addons that track loot (ore, herbs, fish) see it; Maximum is instant but can hide loot from them. Items being rolled on, and the master looter's items to hand out, are left in the loot window. |
| Sell Junk Automatically | Loot & Vendors | Sells your grey items whenever you visit a vendor. Hold Shift to skip. |
| Sell Clutter Automatically | Loot & Vendors | Sells what you pick up while leveling but won't use, whenever you visit a vendor: cooking ingredients and monster parts if no profession of yours uses them, other classes' reagents, gear your class can't use (and cloth armor on Warriors, Rogues and Hunters), drinks on Warriors and Rogues (no mana), food, potions and white gear you've outleveled, and the crafting materials you choose. Valuable materials, items your quests or RestedXP guides need, equipment sets, profession tools and anything that opens are kept. Never sell and Always sell lists fine-tune it, item tooltips say what will be sold, and `/hst clutter list` shows it before you reach a vendor. Hold Shift to skip. |
| Repair Automatically | Loot & Vendors | Repairs your gear whenever you visit a vendor who can repair, from guild funds if you turn that on. Hold Shift to skip. |
| Consumable Macros | Items & Mail | Keeps macros that always use your best food, drink, bandage and potions, updated as your bags change, plus one that eats and drinks at once. Put them on your action bars once from the Macros window. |
| Mail Expiry & Alt List | Items & Mail | Warns you when you log in if mail with items or gold is about to expire on any of your characters, and adds a list of your characters to the To: field when you send mail. |
| Auto-Equip Bags | Items & Mail | When you get a bag, puts it in an empty bag slot, or swaps it for your smallest bag if it has more slots (what was in the old bag moves into the new one). Quivers, ammo pouches and profession bags are left alone. |
| Auto Accept Summons | Group & PvP | Accepts summons from warlocks and meeting stones after a short countdown, waiting until you're out of combat. You can choose to only accept summons from friends, guildmates or community members. |
| Auto Accept Resurrection | Group & PvP | Accepts resurrections from other players as soon as the game allows. Hold Shift to answer them yourself. |
| Auto Release in Battlegrounds | Group & PvP | Releases your spirit as soon as you die in a battleground. Hold Shift to stay. |
| Right-Click Guild Invite | Social | Invite players to your guild straight from the right-click menu on their name in chat (not shown for people already in your guild). |
| Guild Message Popup | Social | Shows your guild's message of the day in the middle of the screen when you log in or when it changes. |
| Flash Taskbar on Whisper | Social | Your WoW icon flashes on the taskbar when someone whispers you while you're tabbed out. |
| Auto-Decline Battle.net Friend Requests | Auto-Decline | Turns down Battle.net friend requests and tells you who sent them. |
| Auto-Decline Duels | Auto-Decline | Turns down duel requests, including duels to the death on Hardcore realms. You can let friends, guildmates or community members through. |
| Auto-Decline Guild Charters | Auto-Decline | Turns down guild charters people ask you to sign. You can let friends, guildmates or community members through. |
| Auto-Decline Join Requests | Auto-Decline | Turns down requests to join your group, and group members' suggestions to invite someone. You can let friends, guildmates or community members through. |
| Auto-Decline Party Invites | Auto-Decline | Turns down group invites. You can let friends, guildmates or community members through. |
| Auto-Decline Shared Quests | Auto-Decline | Turns down quests that group members share with you. You can let friends, guildmates or community members through. |
| Better Tooltips | Interface | Adds a health bar under player and mob tooltips, colors the border by class, and shows guild ranks and notes. Works with other addons' tooltip styles too. |
| Better Scrolling Combat Text | Interface | Replaces the game's scrolling combat text with scroll areas for the damage and healing you take and for notices (combat, low health and mana, spell alerts, combo points, auras, reputation, honor and skill-ups), which can share one area or each get their own; notices you turn off show the game's way. It also puts damage numbers on enemy nameplates or gives the game's own your font and size. Choose fonts, colors, animations and filters, and move the areas in Edit Mode. |
| Better Breath Meter | Interface | Replaces the game's breath bar (and its fatigue and feign death bars) with one that counts down the seconds you have left (tenths for the last ten), turns your warning color and pulses as you run out, and can play a warning sound. Choose its size, look and colors, and move it in Edit Mode. |
| Minimap Button | Interface | A button on the edge of your minimap that opens the settings. Drag it to move it. |
| Hide Issue Reporter | Interface | Removes the Issue Reporter button that the beta client puts on your screen. |
| Mute Mount Sounds | Sound | Silences the noisiest mount and transport sounds: horse hooves, mechanostriders and mechanical footsteps, airships and zeppelins. Choose which in its settings. Turning it off unmutes them, even if another addon muted them too. |
| Mute Custom Sounds | Sound | Mutes any game sounds you choose by their sound file IDs. Its settings can also play sound files, so you can find the ones you want. |

Modules that act for you (Upgrade Spell Ranks, Quests & NPCs, Loot & Vendors, Consumable Macros, Auto-Equip Bags, Group & PvP), the Auto-Decline modules (except Auto-Decline Guild Charters), Better Scrolling Combat Text, Better Breath Meter and the Sound modules start off; everything else starts on. Every module can be turned on or off at any time without a `/reload`. Which modules are on is shared by all characters.

## Settings window

`/hst` opens it. It is also listed under Options › AddOns and in the minimap addon menu. Drag the title bar to move it, the bottom right corner to resize it, and use the − / + beside the close button (or the mouse wheel over them) to scale it; it opens the size, scale and place you left it.

### Sidebar

Search is at the top, then every tweak by category. Click a category to open or close it; its count says how many of its tweaks are on. Profiles and About are at the bottom. The version beside the title opens the patch notes; a blinking dot on it means you haven't opened the newest ones yet (New Patch Dot, at the top of the patch notes, turns it off). Each version there folds open and shut, and a change with an arrow opens its tweak's settings.

### All tweaks

Every tweak with a switch to turn it on or off, filtered by All, On or Off. Pointing at one shows its description and preview on the right; clicking it opens its page.

Tweaks with nothing to set (Kills to Level, Quest XP in Quest Log, Screenshot Level-Ups, Skip NPC Chat, Quest Item Sparkle, Right-Click Guild Invite, Flash Taskbar on Whisper, Auto-Decline Battle.net Friend Requests, Minimap Button, Hide Issue Reporter) have no page. They're just a switch, in the list and in the sidebar, and clicking them turns them on or off.

### A tweak's page

The page has the tweak's switch, tabs for its settings pages (and chips for sub-pages), and the settings in cards, each with its description under it. Sliders take a typed value too. A live preview sits beside the settings (above them for the XP bar) and redraws as you change anything: the XP bar with its labels at its real size, the loot window both as you loot by hand and as auto looting lists it, the tooltip, combat text, breath meter and the rest. "Hide preview" gives the settings the whole width. A tweak that's off shows its settings, disabled, until you turn it on.

### Search

Search finds tweaks and settings by name, description, section, page or menu choice; every word must match. Settings whose name matches come first. A toggle can be switched right in the results; others show their value. The arrow keys move through the results and Enter opens one, highlighting the setting on its page.

### Profiles and About

Profiles lets you switch, create, copy, delete and reset profiles. Profiles hold every tweak's settings; statistics are saved per character. About lists the commands and credits, with GitHub and donate links.

### Other details

Settings that cannot apply are disabled. Font menus preview each font. A confirmation accepted after you switched profiles does nothing, since it was asked about the other profile. Profiles made on one character are listed on all of them. Closing and reopening the window returns to the page you had open. Changes take effect immediately. The Loot Window's "Show on screen" shows its sample where the window opens, to drag it into place.

## Commands

- `/hst`: settings window. `/hst help`: command list.
- `/hst xp`, `/hst tooltip`, `/hst sct`, `/hst breath`, `/hst ranks`, `/hst macros`, `/hst mail`, `/hst clutter`: XP Tracker, Better Tooltips, Better Scrolling Combat Text, Better Breath Meter, Upgrade Spell Ranks, Consumable Macros, Mail Expiry & Alt List and Sell Clutter Automatically settings. Any module's internal name works too, for example `/hst guildmotd`.
- `/hst keep <item>`, `/hst sell <item>`: put an item on Sell Clutter's Never sell or Always sell list (shift-click it into the chat line); again takes it off. `/hst clutter list`: what Sell Clutter would sell right now.

XP bar commands use `/hxp`:

- `/hxp`: XP Tracker settings. `/hxp help`: command list.
- `/hxp lock`, `/hxp unlock`, `/hxp center`: position the XP bar.
- `/hxp preview`: sample XP data for styling the bar at any level.
- `/hxp resetstats`: restart the XP/hour session.
- `/hxp show`, `/hxp hide`: turn the XP Tracker on or off.

## Installing

Unzip the release into your game's `Interface\AddOns` folder, so that you get `Interface\AddOns\HastesSuperTweaks`, then restart the game. Releases are on the project's GitHub page: https://github.com/hastecraft/super-tweaks

## Limits and edge cases

What each module does in unusual situations, and what the game doesn't let addons do.

### Leveling

- XP Tracker takes one settled XP reading per level-up, no work while hidden, and per-character data saved under the real name even when the client says "Unknown" while loading. XP per hour and time to level show "--" for a session's first minute. In dungeons and raids, where the game hides AFK status from addons, "Pause while AFK" goes by the last status it saw. With your XP turned off the bar says "XP OFF". "Hide Blizzard XP bar" hides only the Experience bar, not reputation or honor.
- In Edit Mode the XP bar shows even when its rules would hide it, and snaps to the grid while the grid is shown (Shift places it freely). Addon frames can't join Edit Mode's settings dialog without tainting it, so right-click the bar for its settings. Positions are kept per layout unless "Position per Edit Mode layout" is off; a renamed layout keeps its position.
- Kills to Level reads kill XP from the combat XP message, minus the rested bonus. A kill of the same mob at the same level and group size gives an exact count; anything else is the formula's estimate, marked "~". Pets, totems and guardians get no line.
- Quest XP in Quest Log and the XP bar's quest shading use the game's reward when it's above 0, else Questie's estimate (marked "~"). Forever reports 0 XP for every quest in the log, so without Questie no quest XP shows. Titles update the next time the log redraws; forcing a redraw could taint the quest log.
- Upgrade Spell Ranks only acts on a rank you just learned, and only out of combat with nothing on your cursor (a rank learned in combat goes on your bars when it ends). Macros and items are never changed, and a button is never swapped down. Ranks learned while it's off aren't caught up; use "Upgrade all buttons now".

### Quests, NPCs and group

- Auto Accept & Turn In Quests leaves you quests with a choice of rewards, quests that cost gold or flag you for PvP, repeatable quests, and NPCs with special gossip options. It doesn't accept a share Auto-Decline Shared Quests is declining, or an escort while your quest log is full or you're dead.
- Skip NPC Chat only picks an NPC's single option when it opens a banker, battlemaster, guild charter, tabard, flight, trainer or vendor window. Plain chat, spirit healers, innkeepers and talent unlearning are never chosen.
- Auto Accept Summons checks again when its countdown ends that it's the same summon and that your settings still accept it. A summon in combat is counted down once combat ends; while you can't teleport (falling, say) it waits.
- Auto Accept Resurrection waits out the corpse recovery delay, then checks the offer, Shift and the combat setting again. Hardcore realms offer no resurrection.
- Auto Release in Battlegrounds doesn't release while a soulstone, Reincarnation or someone's resurrection is available, and releases after a `/reload` while dead.

### Loot and vendors

- Sell Junk and Sell Clutter share one sale per vendor visit, one item every tenth of a second: greys first, then clutter, so the Buyback tab holds the clutter. Each item is checked again just before it's sold, a dropped sale is retried once, and only sales that went through are reported. Both skip bags you've set to ignore junk selling and stop when you leave the vendor. Sell Clutter recognizes Classic Era's materials by item ID, since Era files nearly all of them as plain Trade Goods.
- Repair Automatically reports a repair once the game shows it happened. With guild funds on, the game pays what your guild allows and takes the rest from your gold. Short of gold, it waits while you're at the vendor, so junk sold there can pay for it.
- Loot Window hands looting to the game's own window while you're the master looter, since the master loot menus need it. Escape closes the window while it has loot to take; the auto loot list leaves Escape alone. The burnt page's art only loads once that style is used.

### Items and mail

- Consumable Macros recognizes items by their use spell, in the game's language, so new items work without a list; Healthstones are known by item ID. The macros are account-wide, use items by ID, and are only rewritten when their text changes: never in combat, while you're dead, or while the Macros window is open. In combat Eat & Drink leaves out the mana potion after a healing potion, since potions share a cooldown.
- Mail Expiry & Alt List only knows a character's mail once you've opened its mailbox. An inbox holding more than it can show is reported too, since that mail's expiry can't be read until some is taken out.
- Auto-Equip Bags only acts on bags that arrive (looted, bought, or from mail, trade or the bank), never on bags you already had or took off yourself. Quivers, ammo pouches, soul bags and profession bags are never equipped or replaced. It waits out combat and anything on your cursor, and doesn't retry a swap the game refused.

### Social and Auto-Decline

- Right-Click Guild Invite only adds to name menus (chat, channels, communities); unit-frame menus, which hold protected actions, are left alone.
- Guild Message Popup can't read the message in dungeons and raids, where chat is restricted, and shows it at the next chance.
- The Auto-Decline modules turn everyone down unless you let them through on the shared "Let through" section: friends (including Battle.net friends), guild members and community members, all off by default. Characters are matched by GUID, or by name and realm, so a stranger with a friend's name on another realm is still turned down. While chat is locked down (in some instances) the game hides community members, so they count as strangers then.

### Interface

- Better Tooltips works on Blizzard's tooltip and on skinned ones (when another addon hides the border, it draws its own). The guild line replaces a rank another addon added, such as EllesmereUI's. Guild notes come from the guild roster, read at most every 15 seconds; in dungeons and raids, where the game hides the roster, the last notes stay.
- Better Scrolling Combat Text can't read the combat log, which this client closes to addons, so it can't tell which spell hit you or who hit an enemy: numbers on nameplates are everyone's damage to that mob. Mobs tapped by players outside your group are skipped. The game draws nameplates one whole nameplate at a time, so a number can only sit under a nameplate as part of one drawn earlier; by default numbers go behind every nameplate ("Draw numbers" changes that). With "The game's own numbers" chosen for enemies, the game draws its damage numbers itself, so only their font and size change: the font from your next login (the game loads it only then), and the size with the game's other floating text, XP gains included. While it replaces the game's combat text, the game's own frame stops showing what this shows; notices you turn off here still show the game's way, and XP gains stay the game's. The CVars it changes are put back when you turn it off, unless you changed them yourself meanwhile.
- Hide Issue Reporter waits for the reporter to load, and for combat to end if it's protected.
